# Practice Questions

Scenario-based practice question banks, one per certification. Every bank follows the house format: a short scenario, four options, and a collapsible answer with a "why" that also explains the distractors. See [template.md](./template.md) for the format and [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) for how to add one.

**Coverage: every certification on this site has a bank.** When you add a cert, add its bank and list it below.

> These banks are original scenario questions for study. They contain no verbatim vendor exam content.

## How to use

- Read the scenario, commit to an answer, then expand the answer block. The explanation matters more than the letter.
- Weighting sentences at the top of each bank follow the published exam domain blueprint, so time your practice where the marks are.
- Pair a bank with its cert page (linked at the top of each bank) for notes, a practice plan, and strategy.
